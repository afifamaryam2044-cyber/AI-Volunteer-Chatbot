COMMUNITY VOLUNTEER NETWORK - TASK INSTRUCTIONS
FICTIONAL SAMPLE CONTENT FOR AN ACADEMIC PROJECT

Registration desk
1. Arrive 15 minutes before the event and check in.
2. Attend the briefing and confirm which registration materials to use.
3. Welcome each person and ask only for the information requested by the team.
4. Provide the approved badge, schedule, map, or direction.
5. Keep forms and participant information out of public view.
6. Ask the team lead about unclear entries or special requests.
7. At the end of the shift, return records and report the attendance totals
   requested by the team lead.

Event setup
1. Attend the setup briefing before moving equipment or supplies.
2. Check the layout plan with the team lead.
3. Place tables, chairs, signs, and materials as directed.
4. Keep doorways, walkways, and exits clear.
5. Report damaged items, spills, or other hazards immediately.
6. Confirm that the team lead approves the area before visitors enter.

Visitor guidance
1. Wear your volunteer identification badge or vest.
2. Stand in the assigned information area.
3. Greet visitors and ask how you can help.
4. Use the event map or approved information to give directions.
5. If you do not know an answer, contact a team lead instead of guessing.
6. Report accessibility requests, conflicts, or safety concerns promptly.

Distributing materials
1. Confirm which materials and quantities you are allowed to distribute.
2. Keep supplies organized at the assigned table or station.
3. Give materials politely and explain approved instructions when needed.
4. Do not distribute private records or unapproved items.
5. Tell the team lead when supplies are low or damaged.
6. Return unused materials during cleanup.

Event closing
1. Wait for the team lead to announce that closing tasks may begin.
2. Collect reusable materials and return them to their labeled area.
3. Remove approved temporary signs and tidy the assigned space.
4. Report missing, damaged, or unsafe items.
5. Return your badge or vest and any borrowed equipment.
6. Complete check-out and record your departure time.

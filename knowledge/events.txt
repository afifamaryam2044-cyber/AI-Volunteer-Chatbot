COMMUNITY VOLUNTEER NETWORK - SAMPLE EVENT CALENDAR
FICTIONAL SAMPLE CONTENT FOR AN ACADEMIC PROJECT

All times use the local time of the event location. Volunteers should arrive
15 minutes before the listed start time for check-in and briefing.

1. Neighborhood Welcome Fair
- Date: October 3, 2026
- Time: 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM
- Location: Riverside Community Hall
- Purpose: Help residents learn about local volunteer opportunities and
  community services.
- Volunteer roles and tasks:
  - Welcome visitors and guide them to activity tables.
  - Support the registration desk.
  - Distribute event maps and information sheets.
  - Help with room setup and cleanup.

2. Community Garden Workday
- Date: October 10, 2026
- Time: 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
- Location: Greenway Learning Garden
- Purpose: Prepare shared garden areas and teach visitors about community
  gardening.
- Volunteer roles and tasks:
  - Check in participants.
  - Guide small groups to assigned garden areas.
  - Distribute approved tools and safety reminders.
  - Help collect materials and tidy the work area.

3. Family Learning Activity Day
- Date: October 17, 2026
- Time: 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM
- Location: Central Library Activity Room
- Purpose: Provide simple educational activities for families.
- Volunteer roles and tasks:
  - Welcome families and explain the activity layout.
  - Help participants find their assigned stations.
  - Restock activity materials.
  - Report problems or accessibility needs to the team lead.

4. Winter Support Collection
- Date: November 7, 2026
- Time: 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM
- Location: Northside Community Center
- Purpose: Sort donated winter items for distribution through community
  support partners.
- Volunteer roles and tasks:
  - Register volunteers and explain sorting areas.
  - Sort labeled donation boxes under team-lead guidance.
  - Direct visitors to the correct drop-off area.
  - Help count general box totals and clean the workspace.

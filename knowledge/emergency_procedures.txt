COMMUNITY VOLUNTEER NETWORK - EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
FICTIONAL SAMPLE CONTENT FOR AN ACADEMIC PROJECT

Important safety rule
Protect yourself first. For a serious or immediate emergency, contact the
appropriate local emergency services and follow their official instructions.
This document does not provide emergency phone numbers. Do not delay official
help while trying to contact the organization.

General emergency steps
1. Move to a safe location if it is possible to do so without increasing danger.
2. Notify the event lead or Safety and Wellbeing Team.
3. Give a clear location and brief factual description.
4. Follow instructions from emergency services, venue staff, and the event lead.
5. Do not investigate, confront, or physically intervene in a dangerous
   situation unless trained and specifically directed by authorized personnel.
6. Record and report important details only after everyone is safe.

Medical emergency
- Ask someone to notify the event lead and contact the appropriate emergency
  services.
- Keep the area clear and provide simple directions to responders.
- Do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger.
- Do not provide medical treatment beyond your training.
- Protect the person's privacy and keep onlookers away.
- Follow instructions from emergency responders.

Fire or smoke
- Alert people nearby and notify the event lead.
- Contact the appropriate emergency services.
- Leave by the nearest safe exit and follow venue evacuation instructions.
- Do not use elevators or re-enter the building.
- Do not attempt to fight a fire unless you are trained and authorized.
- Gather at the designated assembly area and wait for instructions.

Suspicious or dangerous situation
- Do not approach, touch, move, or confront the suspicious person or item.
- Move away from the area and calmly guide others away if it is safe.
- Notify the event lead and contact the appropriate emergency services.
- Share only factual observations, including the location.
- Do not spread rumors or post details online.

Lost child or person
- Notify the event lead immediately and provide the last known location and
  a description if available.
- Stay with the reporting person in a visible, safe area when appropriate.
- Do not make a public announcement containing private information unless the
  event lead or venue staff instructs you to do so.
- Do not take a child or vulnerable person away from the event area alone.
- Follow instructions from the event lead, venue staff, and emergency services.

Severe weather
- Follow the event lead's instructions and venue safety announcements.
- Move indoors or to the designated safe area when directed.
- Stay away from windows, exposed areas, and damaged structures.
- Do not leave the safe area until the event lead or official authorities say it
  is safe.
- Report injuries or hazards after reaching safety.

Evacuation
- Stop your task when instructed and use the nearest safe exit.
- Walk calmly and help direct visitors without blocking the exit.
- Do not return for personal belongings.
- Go to the designated assembly area and stay with your team.
- Do not re-enter until authorized by emergency services, venue staff, or the
  event lead.
- Tell the team lead if someone may be missing or needs assistance.

Reporting an emergency
- Contact the appropriate emergency services for a serious or immediate threat.
- Notify the event lead as soon as it is safe.
- State what happened, where it happened, and whether anyone may be injured or
  missing.
- Complete an incident report only when the event lead says it is safe and
  appropriate.

COMMUNITY VOLUNTEER NETWORK - VOLUNTEER GUIDELINES
FICTIONAL SAMPLE CONTENT FOR AN ACADEMIC PROJECT

Professional and respectful behavior
- Treat participants, visitors, staff, and other volunteers with respect.
- Be patient, welcoming, and inclusive.
- Do not use insulting, threatening, discriminatory, or aggressive language.
- Follow reasonable instructions from the event coordinator or team lead.

Communication rules
- Speak clearly and politely.
- Share accurate information. If you do not know an answer, ask a team lead
  instead of guessing.
- Use the official event communication channel for schedule or task updates.
- Do not post participant information, private conversations, or event incidents
  on personal social media.

Punctuality
- Arrive at the time stated in the event schedule so that the team can prepare.
- Check in with the registration desk or assigned team lead when you arrive.
- Tell the coordinator as soon as possible if you expect to be late.

Teamwork
- Cooperate with volunteers from all teams.
- Offer help when your assigned task is complete.
- Ask for clarification before starting an unfamiliar task.
- Report problems promptly and do not leave an assigned area without telling
  the team lead.

Dress and identification
- Wear clean, comfortable clothing suitable for the event location and weather.
- Wear the volunteer identification badge or vest provided for the event.
- Use safe, closed-toe footwear when setup or moving materials is required.
- Do not wear clothing or symbols that could make participants feel unsafe or
  unwelcome.

Confidentiality
- Keep participant names, registration details, and personal circumstances
  confidential.
- Collect only information requested by the event team.
- Do not copy, photograph, or share registration records.
- Return paper forms and other confidential materials to the team lead.

General volunteer responsibilities
- Complete assigned tasks carefully and safely.
- Follow the attendance rules and task instructions.
- Keep shared spaces tidy.
- Report injuries, hazards, lost persons, damaged equipment, or other concerns
  to the coordinator immediately.
- Take part in the check-out process before leaving.

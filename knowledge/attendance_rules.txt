COMMUNITY VOLUNTEER NETWORK - ATTENDANCE RULES
FICTIONAL SAMPLE CONTENT FOR AN ACADEMIC PROJECT

Check-in
- Arrive 15 minutes before the event start time.
- Check in at the registration desk or with the assigned team lead.
- Record your name, arrival time, and assigned team as requested.
- Attend the volunteer briefing before beginning a task.

Check-out
- Tell the team lead when your assigned work is complete.
- Return badges, vests, keys, forms, and other shared materials.
- Record your departure time at the registration desk or with the team lead.
- Do not leave an event without checking out, except during an emergency.

Punctuality
- Volunteers are expected to arrive on time and remain for their agreed shift.
- Arriving 15 minutes early supports setup and briefing.
- If you are delayed, contact the event coordinator or team lead as soon as
  possible and provide your expected arrival time.

Late arrival
- A volunteer who arrives after the scheduled start must check in before
  joining a task.
- The team lead may assign a different task if the original briefing or task
  has already started.
- Do not enter an active task area without checking with the team lead.

Absence
- If you cannot attend, inform the event coordinator as early as possible.
- For a planned absence, notify the coordinator before the event day.
- For an unexpected absence, use the official event communication channel when
  it is safe and practical to do so.
- Do not ask another person to sign in for you.

Attendance records
- Attendance records are maintained by the registration desk or team lead.
- Records include check-in and check-out times and may include the assigned
  team.
- Volunteers may ask the coordinator to correct an honest recording error.
- Attendance information is used for volunteer administration and is kept
  confidential.

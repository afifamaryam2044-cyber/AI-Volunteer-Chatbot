COMMUNITY VOLUNTEER NETWORK - COMMON VOLUNTEER DUTIES
FICTIONAL SAMPLE CONTENT FOR AN ACADEMIC PROJECT

Registration
- Welcome volunteers and participants at the registration desk.
- Confirm the information requested by the event team.
- Give badges, schedules, or directions when instructed.
- Keep registration records private and return them to the team lead.

Helping participants
- Explain the activity layout using approved event information.
- Help participants find a station, room, or waiting area.
- Offer practical assistance within your training and assigned role.
- Ask a team lead for help when a request is outside your role.

Event setup
- Arrive early enough for the setup briefing.
- Place signs, tables, chairs, materials, and supplies as directed.
- Check that walkways and exits are not blocked.
- Report damaged equipment or hazards before the event begins.

Event cleanup
- Return materials to their labeled storage area.
- Collect waste and leave activity spaces tidy.
- Remove temporary signs only when the team lead approves.
- Report missing or damaged items during check-out.

Guiding visitors
- Welcome visitors and provide clear directions.
- Use event maps or signs rather than guessing.
- Escort visitors only when this is part of the assigned task.
- Tell the team lead if a visitor needs accessibility support.

Distributing materials
- Give out only the materials approved for that event.
- Explain how materials should be used when instructions are provided.
- Keep extra supplies organized and report low stock.
- Do not distribute confidential records or unapproved items.

Reporting problems
- Report injuries, hazards, conflicts, lost persons, suspicious behavior,
  missing materials, or equipment problems immediately.
- Give the team lead the location and a brief factual description.
- Do not investigate dangerous situations alone.

Assisting the team
- Attend the briefing and follow the assigned schedule.
- Support other volunteers after finishing your own task.
- Ask before changing locations or duties.
- Complete check-out and share important updates before leaving.
